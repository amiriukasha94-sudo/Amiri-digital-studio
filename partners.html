CLEAR OWNERSHIP. CLEAR PERMISSIONS.

What you control — and what remains ours.

You retain

Ownership of the original platform source code, core software, infrastructure, platform brand and underlying intellectual property remains with Amiri Digital Studio, except third-party components and rights expressly stated otherwise.

You are licensed to use

Your signed licence grants defined rights to operate the platform, display your approved branding, serve customers and use specified features for the agreed term and territory.

You manage

Your own customers, staff, pricing and service delivery within the contract. Customer data access, export, retention and deletion responsibilities must be defined in the agreement.

Amiri centrally manages

Core platform updates, licence status, platform security and agreed technical support. Suspension or termination must follow the signed agreement and applicable law.

Important: A partner licence is not a sale of the website, source code, company, domain or intellectual property. We will state this plainly in the proposal and contract. Do not represent the licensed business as an acquisition or claim exclusive ownership.
